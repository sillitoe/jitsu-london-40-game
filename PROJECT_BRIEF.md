# Project Brief

## Working Title

TJJF 40th Anniversary Rhythm Game

## Purpose

Create a browser-first rhythm action game celebrating the TJJF 40th anniversary. The game should feel like a playful, affectionate grading-room tribute: recognisable Jitsu etiquette and grading atmosphere, comic timing, instructor advice shouted over the action, and examiner feedback after each exchange.

## Core Fantasy

The player is a student at a grading. An attacker steps in. The instructor calls advice in rhythm. The player hits the right sequence at the right time, causing evasions, turns, guards, and throws. Good timing looks sharp and satisfying. Bad timing causes exaggerated, comedic contact and examiner notes.

## Setting

The game takes place in a grading hall. Two examiners, Steve and Gareth, sit behind a table watching the grading. Steve talks a lot. Gareth says very little.

## Required Characters

- Student: player character, viewed clearly enough to show belt, uniform, and right-shoulder badge.
- Attacker: varied opponents who approach from the front or side.
- Instructor: gives rhythm-based advice.
- Examiners: Steve and Gareth at the grading table.

## Visual Rules

Every character has exactly one square "Jitsu" badge. It is always on the wearer's right shoulder only.

Badge orientation rule:
- If a character is facing the camera, their right shoulder appears on the viewer's left.
- If a character is facing away from the camera, their right shoulder appears on the viewer's right.

Examiners:
- Steve and Gareth wear white judogi.
- They wear black waistcoats including black sleeves.
- They wear black hakama.
- They have one square red badge with white writing on the right shoulder.

Instructors:
- Instructors wear white judogi.
- They wear black waistcoats.
- They wear black hakama.
- They have one square white badge with red writing on the right shoulder.

Students:
- Students wear white judogi.
- Belt colour shows current grade.
- They have one square white badge with black writing on the right shoulder.

Cast direction:
- Students and attackers should include a deliberate mix of genders, skin colours, hair colours, body shapes, and sizes.

## Grades

The grade ladder is:

1. White
2. Yellow
3. Orange
4. Green
5. Purple
6. Light blue
7. Dark blue
8. Brown
9. Black

## Gameplay Pillars

- Rhythm-based instructor advice.
- Successful timing creates evasions, turns, guards, and throws.
- Failure results in comedic hits.
- Local two-player split screen.
- Wisdom or special meter that rewards strong timing and lets the player trigger a special grading-room flourish.
- Examiner feedback after each exchange.
- Browser-first deployment.

## v0.1 Vertical Slice

Build the smallest test of whether the idea is fun:

- One grading room.
- Steve and Gareth at the table.
- One instructor.
- One student.
- One attacker.
- Three callout patterns.
- Timing judgement: PERFECT, GOOD, LATE, MISS.
- Visible score, combo, wisdom meter, and examiner feedback.
- Keyboard input first.
- Split-screen structure present, even if player two mirrors placeholder behaviour initially.
